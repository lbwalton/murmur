# Security policy

## How do I report a security problem in murmur?

Email security@murmurapp.app. Please do not open a public issue, so a problem can be fixed before it is widely known. The same address is in [security.txt](https://murmurapp.app/.well-known/security.txt).

Say what you found, how to reproduce it, and which version and platform you used. A short proof is more useful than a scanner's output.

## What is in scope?

- The murmur desktop app for macOS and Windows
- murmur for iPhone
- murmurapp.app
- This repository and how releases are built and published

Your speech or cleanup provider is its own company, so report a problem with their service to them. Denial of service tests, social engineering, and scanner results with no working problem behind them are out of scope.

## What happens after I report?

You get a reply within 3 business days saying whether the problem is confirmed. Once it is, you get word when the fix ships, and credit if you would like it. murmur is made by a small company and has no bug bounty. Good faith research that follows this policy will not face legal action.

## Which versions get security fixes?

The latest release. murmur updates itself by default, so fixes reach most copies on their own. With Updates set to Ask first it takes a click, and with Off nothing arrives until you update by hand.
