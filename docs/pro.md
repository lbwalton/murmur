# murmur Pro

## What is murmur Pro?

A one-time purchase that supports murmur's development. The free app is complete and stays complete: dictation, formatting, the journey, every provider. Pro adds conveniences on top and a supporter mark, and it funds the work. Belts and cosmetics are earned by practice and are never for sale.

## What does Pro include?

- Provider profiles: save whole provider setups (endpoint and models; the key ring supplies each provider's saved key automatically) under names and switch between them in one click.
- Early access: new conveniences unlock for Pro first in official builds, then go free for everyone after a window. The source is public the entire time.
- Priority support and, if you like, your name in SUPPORTERS.md.

## Where does the Pro mark appear, and what does it mean?

The Pro mark is a small gold pill that says PRO. **Filled means Pro is yours; outlined means Pro offers something there.**

- **With Pro:** a filled mark sits right after the murmur wordmark at the top of the window, before your belt, beside murmur Pro in the settings rail and in the murmur Pro section title, and on the Profiles row under Provider and keys. Founding members wear the same mark, and their Pro section says Founding member.
- **Without Pro:** an outlined mark appears only where Pro adds something: the Profiles row under Provider and keys, the murmur Pro entry in the rail, and the murmur Pro section title. There is no mark at the top of the window. A deactivated key counts as free until you reactivate it.

Nothing else in the app grows a mark, and Home never shows a standing one.

## What happens to the Pro mark when I open murmur each day?

The first time each day the murmur window opens in front of you, the Pro mark at the top lifts to twice its size, 3 or 4 slashing brush strokes paint its gold in from different angles, and it settles back into place. It takes about 4 seconds, and your belt steps aside while the mark is big. In a narrow window, the mark grows only as far as the room before the tabs. The strokes stay exactly as they landed, dry ends and small gaps showing, as that day's badge; each day's strokes are different, seeded by the date.

- Opening the window again the same day shows today's badge still.
- A click, a key press, or switching to another app skips straight to the end.
- Press the mark anytime to see today's strokes painted close up again (with reduce motion on, pressing it does nothing).
- To turn it off, go to Settings, murmur Pro, and switch off **Paint the mark each day**. Off keeps today's brushwork still.
- With reduce motion turned on in your system settings, the mark shows the day's finished brushwork with no lift and no animation.

## What happens when I activate Pro?

When Activate or Reactivate succeeds, the outlined marks fill with gold one after another, and the mark at the top arrives with its brush fill (that counts as the day's fill). The next time you open Home, it says thank you, once.

## Will murmur ask me to buy Pro?

Once, quietly. 30 calendar days after you install murmur, Home shows one line under back today with how much time murmur has given you back so far, and two buttons: **See Pro** and **No thanks**. Either one, or buying Pro, dismisses it for good, and it never shows to anyone with a key, active or deactivated. Installs from before murmur kept an install date count from their earliest dictation on record.

## What is a founding member?

Everyone who buys Pro with the code FOUNDERS before the founders window ends, at the end of December 31, 2026, is a founding member forever. The code takes $10 off at the Stripe checkout, so Pro is $39 instead of $49; it works only there, never in the App Store, and stops working when the window ends. Founding members keep a permanent ten percent discount on every future paid product, including the cloud subscription and any paid iOS offering, and their license shows the founding member mark in settings.

## Does murmur Pro cover my iPhone too?

Yes, either way you buy it. Apple does not let an iPhone app unlock anything with a license key, so it works by email:

- **Bought Pro on your Mac or PC?** Email support@murmurapp.app with your order id, from the address on your license. You get a one-time App Store code for murmur Pro on iPhone at no cost; on your iPhone, open murmur, tap the gear, then murmur Pro, and tap Redeem a code. Apple's codes last at most 6 months, so ask when you are ready. Founding members get a founders code, so their iPhone shows Founding member too.
- **Bought Pro on your iPhone?** Email support@murmurapp.app with the order id from Apple's email receipt. Once Apple confirms the purchase, you get a license key for the Mac and Windows app.

(Checked 2026-10-22 against Apple's App Review Guidelines, 3.1.1, and App Store Connect Help on offer codes.)

## How do license keys work?

Your key is a cryptographic signature that murmur verifies on your machine, offline. There is no licensing server, no account, and no phone-home: if murmur's servers vanished tomorrow, your key would keep working forever. Activate it under Settings, murmur Pro by pasting the key and clicking Activate.

## How do I deactivate Pro, and can I come back without my key?

Yes. Click **Deactivate** under Settings, murmur Pro: the app switches to the free experience, and your key stays parked on this machine. **Reactivate** brings everything back in one click, no key hunting. This is handy for previewing what free users see, or for troubleshooting.

**Forget key** is different: it deletes the key from this machine entirely, active or parked. Use it when you are selling or handing off the computer. After Forget key, activating again means pasting your original key (see the lost-key answer below if you no longer have it).

## I lost my key. What do I do?

Email support with your order id or receipt and a fresh key is issued. Keys are free to reissue for the same purchase.
