murmur is a keyboard you talk to. Tap the pill, say what you mean, tap again, and your words land in whatever app you are in, cleaned up: fillers gone, punctuation in.

It is free and open source (GPLv3), with no account. murmur runs on your own API key: paste a free Groq key once, and you pay your provider directly, usually cents a month. Your voice goes from your iPhone to the provider you pick, and nowhere else. murmur collects nothing.

What it does
- The murmur keyboard: a dictation pad in every app. It types only the words you dictate and makes no network requests of its own.
- Cleanup you choose: Off, Light, or Full. Full adds spoken punctuation, scratch that, numbers, and a polish pass by a cleanup model. If polish fails, chats, or replies to what you said, murmur types your words instead.
- Lists and corrections when you want them: turn on Smart lists, say first, then, and then, and get a numbered list. Turn on Apply corrections, say Tuesday, oh wait, I mean Wednesday, and only Wednesday is typed.
- Notes by voice: keep a take as a note instead of typing it, in murmur or in your notes vault. Turn on sorting and murmur files your tasks and ideas, with reminders for tasks.
- Transform: select text, say how to change it, and murmur rewrites it.
- Your words: a dictionary for names spelled your way, and expansions for things you say often.
- Start anywhere: Control Center, the Action Button, and the Lock Screen.
- History stays on your iPhone, and you can delete it at any time.

Practice, painted
Every take counts toward belts and stripes, like a martial art, earned by practice and never sold. Each day gets a painting that grows a stroke with every take and is sealed in your belt's color. The Journey shows your path, and a daily recap tells you how much time talking gave you back.

Any provider
Groq is the default, or use any OpenAI-compatible service, such as OpenAI or Mistral. Your keys stay in your iPhone's Keychain.

murmur Pro
An optional one-time purchase that supports murmur and adds provider profiles, early access, and the supporter mark. It covers murmur on your Mac or PC too. Everything above stays free.

murmur is also on Mac and Windows at murmurapp.app.
