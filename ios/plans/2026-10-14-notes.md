# Notes (IOS-023) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Brain dump on the phone. A take is kept as a note instead of typed: from the keyboard's pill (Note), from New note in murmur, or from New note on the icon, in Control Center, or on the Action Button. It lands in the day's inbox file, desktop's markdown, word for word. With sorting on, desktop's sorter files its tasks and ideas through your cleanup connection, holds the lines it is unsure of for you, and task reminders say what is still open. The notes live in murmur's own storage, or in a folder you pick, such as your Obsidian vault.

**Architecture:**
- **Desktop's rules, shared.** The sorter's prompts, numbers, and file layout move from desktop's TypeScript into `shared/sorter.json`, which desktop now reads; its behavior does not change. Two desktop tests pin desktop's own answers into `shared/notes-vectors.json` (the pure note rules over many inputs) and `shared/sort-scenarios.json` (35 whole sorts with the model's replies, the requests sent, and the files left). Both platforms run both files.
- **MurmurCore ports.** Desktop's `shared/notes.ts`, `sorter.ts`, `compare.ts`, `todo.ts`, `receipt.ts`, and `main/notes/sorter.ts` become `NoteFiles`, `Sorter`, `Compare`, `Todo`, `Receipt`, and `NoteSort`, with `JSValue` for JavaScript's string and regex behavior. `NoteSort.chat` is desktop's `askModel` over the cleanup connection.
- **The app.** `NoteStore` is the notes folder: murmur's own, out of backups, or a folder you picked, reached through its bookmark and coordinated with Obsidian and iCloud. `Notes` saves to the inbox first, then queues one sort at a time, holding background time, and keeps the newest note and held lines across launches. `TaskReminders` lays the week's reminders down ahead. The keyboard's pill menu gains Note; the mailbox carries it to the bridge, which saves the words and tells the keyboard Noted to inbox, or types them when the note cannot be saved.
- **The screens.** Home gains a Notes row and New note. Notes shows the newest note and its sort, Held for you with desktop's Tick it, File it anyway, and Skip, the tasks and ideas, and every inbox day, each shareable as text. Settings, Notes has the folder, sorting, and reminders.
- **No hotkey on iPhone, so:** New note on a press and hold of murmur's icon, a New note control for Control Center, the Lock Screen, and the Action Button, and New murmur note in Shortcuts. Each opens New note already listening. No link can, since any app can open a link.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6, Swift Testing, SwiftUI, UIKit (the keyboard, quick actions), AppIntents and WidgetKit (the control), UserNotifications, Foundation's NSFileCoordinator and security-scoped bookmarks, TypeScript and vitest.

## Rulings in this plan

LaBroi can overturn any of these; each says what it costs if wrong.

1. **Notes live in murmur's own storage, out of iCloud backups, until you pick a folder.** Picking one (your Obsidian vault) writes desktop's same files there; notes already written stay where they were, and Keep notes in murmur goes back. If wrong: a move-my-notes button later.
2. **Sorting starts off, and reminders start off at 8:30 AM, 1:00 PM, and 5:30 PM,** desktop's defaults (`src/shared/settings.ts`, read 2026-10-14). Off, a note's words never leave the phone for sorting. If wrong: two constants.
3. **The iPhone sorts by chat only, through the cleanup connection,** at `sorter.json`'s temperature and time limit; the decision model stays on the Mac. As polish does, the time limit is URLRequest's (no data for 12 seconds), where desktop aborts the whole request at 12 seconds. If wrong: a task timer around the request.
4. **Topic headings stay off on iPhone** (desktop's default), and Tick it keeps no backup copy on iPhone. If wrong: a setting and a copy.
5. **The newest note and held lines persist** in Application Support/notes-state, out of backups.
6. **Reminders are laid down a week ahead,** and again whenever murmur changes your tasks and each time murmur opens. A box ticked in Obsidian or on the Mac reaches the reminders at the next open.
7. **A note the keyboard cannot save is typed instead,** so the words always land somewhere.
8. **New note opens listening** from the icon, the control, the Action Button, and Shortcuts; Home's and Notes' New note open the screen with its button. No link starts it.
9. **A folder murmur can no longer reach reads nothing and writes nothing.** The keyboard types the note instead, and Notes and Settings, Notes say so with Choose folder. Notes never fall back to murmur's own storage silently, which would split them across two places.
10. **Every read and write goes through NSFileCoordinator,** and an append reads and writes inside one coordinated write, so Obsidian or iCloud never sees half a file.
11. **Both devices writing the same day's file through iCloud Drive can lose one version;** the docs say so and say Obsidian Sync merges. There is no per-device inbox file, so both devices keep one task list.
12. **A sort holds background time from iOS** until its files are written.
13. **Note in the pill's menu is for the next take only,** remembered in the keyboard's own defaults until that take starts; the menu then reads Type the next take.
14. **The vector tests regenerate their files with `MURMUR_WRITE_VECTORS=1`;** without it they only compare.

// SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-3.0-only
// JSON as JavaScript reads it (ios/prd.json IOS-023). Desktop's sorter
// reads a model's reply with JSON.parse and walks it with Object.entries
// and Number(key), and the reply decides what files; Foundation's
// JSONSerialization keeps no key order and its own idea of a repeated
// key. This keeps JavaScript's: an object's keys come back with the
// array-index keys first, ascending, then the rest in the order first
// written, and a repeated key keeps its first place and its last value.
import Foundation

/// A value as JSON.parse gives it.
public indirect enum JSValue: Equatable, Sendable {
    case null
    case bool(Bool)
    case number(Double)
    case string(String)
    case array([JSValue])
    case object(JSObject)

    /// JSON.parse, or nil where it would throw.
    public static func parse(_ text: String) -> JSValue? {
        var reader = Reader(units: Array(text.utf16))
        reader.skipSpace()
        guard let value = reader.value() else { return nil }
        reader.skipSpace()
        return reader.atEnd ? value : nil
    }

    public var object: JSObject? {
        if case let .object(object) = self { return object }
        return nil
    }

    public var array: [JSValue]? {
        if case let .array(array) = self { return array }
        return nil
    }

    public var number: Double? {
        if case let .number(number) = self { return number }
        return nil
    }

    public var string: String? {
        if case let .string(string) = self { return string }
        return nil
    }
}

/// An object, its keys in Object.entries order. Keys are compared by
/// UTF-16 code units, as JavaScript compares them.
public struct JSObject: Equatable, Sendable {
    private var order: [[UInt16]] = []
    private var values: [[UInt16]: JSValue] = [:]

    public init() {}

    public subscript(key: String) -> JSValue? {
        values[Array(key.utf16)]
    }

    mutating func set(_ key: [UInt16], _ value: JSValue) {
        if values[key] == nil { order.append(key) }
        values[key] = value
    }

    /// Object.entries: array-index keys ascending, then the rest as written.
    public var entries: [(key: String, value: JSValue)] {
        let indexes = order.compactMap { key in JSObject.arrayIndex(key).map { (key, $0) } }.sorted { $0.1 < $1.1 }.map(\.0)
        let rest = order.filter { JSObject.arrayIndex($0) == nil }
        return (indexes + rest).compactMap { key in values[key].map { (String(decoding: key, as: UTF16.self), $0) } }
    }

    public var count: Int { order.count }

    /// A key that is an array index: a canonical whole number below 2^32 - 1.
    static func arrayIndex(_ key: [UInt16]) -> UInt32? {
        guard !key.isEmpty, key.count <= 10, key.allSatisfy({ $0 >= 0x30 && $0 <= 0x39 }) else { return nil }
        if key.count > 1 && key.first == 0x30 { return nil }
        guard let n = UInt64(String(decoding: key, as: UTF16.self)), n < 4_294_967_295 else { return nil }
        return UInt32(n)
    }
}

private struct Reader {
    let units: [UInt16]
    var at = 0

    var atEnd: Bool { at >= units.count }

    mutating func skipSpace() {
        while at < units.count, [0x20, 0x09, 0x0A, 0x0D].contains(units[at]) { at += 1 }
    }

    mutating func value() -> JSValue? {
        guard at < units.count else { return nil }
        switch units[at] {
        case 0x7B: return object()
        case 0x5B: return array()
        case 0x22: return string().map(JSValue.string)
        case 0x74: return literal("true", .bool(true))
        case 0x66: return literal("false", .bool(false))
        case 0x6E: return literal("null", .null)
        default: return number()
        }
    }

    mutating func literal(_ word: String, _ value: JSValue) -> JSValue? {
        let wanted = Array(word.utf16)
        guard at + wanted.count <= units.count, Array(units[at..<(at + wanted.count)]) == wanted else { return nil }
        at += wanted.count
        return value
    }

    mutating func object() -> JSValue? {
        at += 1
        var object = JSObject()
        skipSpace()
        if at < units.count, units[at] == 0x7D {
            at += 1
            return .object(object)
        }
        while true {
            skipSpace()
            guard at < units.count, units[at] == 0x22, let key = string() else { return nil }
            skipSpace()
            guard at < units.count, units[at] == 0x3A else { return nil }
            at += 1
            skipSpace()
            guard let value = value() else { return nil }
            object.set(Array(key.utf16), value)
            skipSpace()
            guard at < units.count else { return nil }
            if units[at] == 0x2C {
                at += 1
                continue
            }
            guard units[at] == 0x7D else { return nil }
            at += 1
            return .object(object)
        }
    }

    mutating func array() -> JSValue? {
        at += 1
        var items: [JSValue] = []
        skipSpace()
        if at < units.count, units[at] == 0x5D {
            at += 1
            return .array(items)
        }
        while true {
            skipSpace()
            guard let item = value() else { return nil }
            items.append(item)
            skipSpace()
            guard at < units.count else { return nil }
            if units[at] == 0x2C {
                at += 1
                continue
            }
            guard units[at] == 0x5D else { return nil }
            at += 1
            return .array(items)
        }
    }

    mutating func string() -> String? {
        at += 1
        var out: [UInt16] = []
        while at < units.count {
            let unit = units[at]
            at += 1
            switch unit {
            case 0x22:
                return String(decoding: out, as: UTF16.self)
            case 0x5C:
                guard at < units.count else { return nil }
                let escape = units[at]
                at += 1
                switch escape {
                case 0x22, 0x5C, 0x2F: out.append(escape)
                case 0x62: out.append(0x08)
                case 0x66: out.append(0x0C)
                case 0x6E: out.append(0x0A)
                case 0x72: out.append(0x0D)
                case 0x74: out.append(0x09)
                case 0x75:
                    guard at + 4 <= units.count, let code = UInt16(String(decoding: units[at..<(at + 4)], as: UTF16.self), radix: 16) else { return nil }
                    out.append(code)
                    at += 4
                default: return nil
                }
            default:
                if unit < 0x20 { return nil }
                out.append(unit)
            }
        }
        return nil
    }

    mutating func number() -> JSValue? {
        let start = at
        func digits() -> Int {
            let from = at
            while at < units.count, units[at] >= 0x30, units[at] <= 0x39 { at += 1 }
            return at - from
        }
        if at < units.count, units[at] == 0x2D { at += 1 }
        guard at < units.count else { return nil }
        if units[at] == 0x30 {
            at += 1
        } else if digits() == 0 {
            return nil
        }
        if at < units.count, units[at] == 0x2E {
            at += 1
            guard digits() > 0 else { return nil }
        }
        if at < units.count, units[at] == 0x65 || units[at] == 0x45 {
            at += 1
            if at < units.count, units[at] == 0x2B || units[at] == 0x2D { at += 1 }
            guard digits() > 0 else { return nil }
        }
        return Double(String(decoding: units[start..<at], as: UTF16.self)).map(JSValue.number)
    }
}

extension JS {
    /// Number(text): JavaScript's string to number, NaN where it gives NaN.
    static func number(_ text: String) -> Double {
        let trimmed = trim(text)
        if trimmed.isEmpty { return 0 }
        switch trimmed {
        case "Infinity", "+Infinity": return .infinity
        case "-Infinity": return -.infinity
        default: break
        }
        let units = Array(trimmed.utf16)
        if units.count > 2, units[0] == 0x30 {
            let radix: Int? = switch units[1] {
            case 0x78, 0x58: 16
            case 0x6F, 0x4F: 8
            case 0x62, 0x42: 2
            default: nil
            }
            if let radix {
                var value = 0.0
                for unit in units.dropFirst(2) {
                    guard let digit = Int(String(UnicodeScalar(UInt8(truncatingIfNeeded: unit))), radix: radix), unit < 0x80 else { return .nan }
                    value = value * Double(radix) + Double(digit)
                }
                return value
            }
        }
        let decimal = regex("^[+-]?(?:[0-9]+\\.?[0-9]*|\\.[0-9]+)(?:[eE][+-]?[0-9]+)?\(end)")
        guard decimal.firstMatch(in: trimmed, range: NSRange(location: 0, length: units.count)) != nil else { return .nan }
        return Double(trimmed) ?? .nan
    }

    static func isInteger(_ x: Double) -> Bool {
        x.isFinite && x.rounded(.towardZero) == x
    }

// The note sorter's pure half (ios/prd.json IOS-023): desktop's
// src/shared/sorter.ts in Swift. The model only labels numbered
// sentences, votes on cut points the code found, and points at word
// numbers; murmur copies the spoken words into the files itself, so

import Foundation

/// A lead-in: the words that introduce a run of items ("I need to buy"),
/// filed once with the items nested under it. group tells two runs with

public enum Sorter {
    /// The units the model labels, and the lead-in each list line sits
    /// under: one unit per line, a prose line cut at its sentence ends, a
    /// list line kept whole, and a prose line ending in a colon right above
    /// list lines taken as their lead-in instead of a unit.
    public static func splitUnits(_ text: String) -> (sentences: [String], leads: [LeadIn?]) {
        var sentences: [String] = []
        var leads: [LeadIn?] = []
        let lines = text.components(separatedBy: "\n").map(JS.trim).filter { !$0.isEmpty }
        var heading: LeadIn?
        for (index, line) in lines.enumerated() {
            if test(listLine, line) {
                sentences.append(line)
                leads.append(heading)
                continue
            }
            heading = nil
            let headsList = index + 1 < lines.count && test(listLine, lines[index + 1])
            let pieces = split(line, sentenceEnd).map(JS.trim).filter { !$0.isEmpty }
            var carry = ""
            var out: [String] = []
            for piece in pieces {
                let joined = carry.isEmpty ? piece : "\(carry) \(piece)"
                if test(abbreviation, joined) {
                    carry = joined
                    continue
                }
                out.append(joined)
                carry = ""
            }
            if !carry.isEmpty { out.append(carry) }
            if headsList, let last = out.last, last.utf16.last == 0x3A {
                out.removeLast()
                heading = LeadIn(text: last, group: "heading-\(index)")
            }
            for sentence in out {
                sentences.append(sentence)
                leads.append(nil)
            }
        }
        return (sentences, leads)
    }

    /// The sentences, numbered from one, as the model receives them.
    public static func numberedList(_ sentences: [String]) -> String {
        sentences.enumerated().map { "\($0.offset + 1). \($0.element)" }.joined(separator: "\n")

    /// Whether a reply is a complete labeling of count sentences: every
    /// number exactly once, only the three labels. The seam, relation, and
    /// start votes ride the same object under reserved keys, allowed only
    /// when offered; a bad vote is no vote for that sentence alone.
    public static func validate(_ count: Int, _ candidate: String, seamCounts: [Int] = [], items: Int = 0, leadCounts: [Int] = []) -> SortReading {
        let text = JS.trim(candidate)
        if text.isEmpty { return .rejected("empty") }
        guard let parsed = JSValue.parse(extractObject(text) ?? text) else { return .rejected("not json") }
        guard let object = parsed.object else { return .rejected("not an object") }
        let offeredSeams = seamCounts.contains { $0 > 0 }
        let offeredItems = items > 0
        let offeredLeads = leadCounts.contains { $0 > 0 }
        var reserved: Set<String> = []
        if offeredSeams { reserved.insert("seams") }
        if offeredItems { reserved.insert("relations") }
        if offeredLeads { reserved.insert("starts") }
        let expected = Set((0..<max(0, count)).map { String($0 + 1) })
        let numbered = object.entries.filter { !reserved.contains($0.key) }
        for entry in numbered where !expected.contains(entry.key) {
            return .rejected("extra sentences")
        }
        if numbered.count < count { return .rejected("missing sentences") }
        var labels: [SortLabel] = []
        for i in stride(from: 1, through: count, by: 1) {
            let label = object[String(i)]?.string.map { JS.trim($0).lowercased() } ?? ""
            guard let known = SortLabel(rawValue: label) else { return .rejected("bad label") }
            labels.append(known)
        }
        let seams = offeredSeams ? readSeamVotes(object["seams"], seamCounts: seamCounts) : [:]
        let relations = offeredItems ? Compare.readRelationVotes(object["relations"], sentenceCount: count, itemCount: items) : [:]
        let starts = offeredLeads ? readLeadStarts(object["starts"], leadCounts: leadCounts) : nil
        return .labeled(SortVerdict(labels: labels, seams: seams, relations: relations, starts: starts))
    }

    /// The text from the first { to the last }, fences and chatter dropped.
    static func extractObject(_ candidate: String) -> String? {
        let source = candidate as NSString
        let start = source.range(of: "{", options: .literal).location
        let end = source.range(of: "}", options: [.literal, .backwards]).location
        guard start != NSNotFound, end != NSNotFound, end > start else { return nil }
        return source.substring(with: NSRange(location: start, length: end - start + 1))
    }

    /// Candidate seams, in order: none in a list line, none where either
    /// side has no words of its own, none inside a quote or parenthesis.
    public static func findSeams(_ sentence: String) -> [Seam] {
        if test(listLine, sentence) { return [] }
        let source = sentence as NSString

        for match in seam.matches(in: sentence, range: NSRange(location: 0, length: source.length)) {

            let right = source.substring(from: end)
            guard hasWords(left), hasWords(right), !insideQuoteOrParen(left) else { continue }
            seams.append(Seam(start: start, end: end))

        return seams

    public static func sides(_ sentence: String, _ seam: Seam) -> (left: String, right: String) {

        return (JS.trim(source.substring(to: seam.start)), JS.trim(source.substring(from: seam.end)))

    /// Cut at the confirmed seams only; a vote for a seam that does not
    /// exist leaves the whole sentence as one line.
    public static func cut(_ sentence: String, _ seams: [Seam], confirmed: [Int]) -> [String] {
        if confirmed.contains(where: { $0 < 1 || $0 > seams.count }) { return [sentence] }
        var seen: [Int] = []
        for n in confirmed where !seen.contains(n) { seen.append(n) }
        let chosen = seen.map { seams[$0 - 1] }.sorted { $0.start < $1.start }
        let source = sentence as NSString
        var pieces: [String] = []
        var cursor = 0
        for seam in chosen {
            pieces.append(source.substring(with: NSRange(location: cursor, length: seam.start - cursor)))
            cursor = seam.end
        }
        pieces.append(source.substring(from: cursor))
        return pieces.map { JS.trim(JS.replace(JS.trim($0), trailingJoiners, with: "")) }.filter { !$0.isEmpty }
    }

    /// A vote as a clean, sorted list of seam numbers, or nil when it is
    /// not a list of whole numbers naming real seams.
    static func confirmedSeams(_ vote: JSValue?, seamCount: Int) -> [Int]? {
        guard let list = vote?.array else { return nil }
        var numbers: [Int] = []
        for value in list {
            guard let n = value.number, JS.isInteger(n), n >= 1, n <= Double(seamCount) else { return nil }
            if !numbers.contains(Int(n)) { numbers.append(Int(n)) }
        }
        return numbers.sorted()
    }

    static func readSeamVotes(_ raw: JSValue?, seamCounts: [Int]) -> [Int: [Int]] {
        var votes: [Int: [Int]] = [:]
        guard let object = raw?.object else { return votes }
        for (key, value) in object.entries {
            let sentence = JS.number(key)
            guard JS.isInteger(sentence), sentence >= 1, sentence <= Double(seamCounts.count) else { continue }
            let count = seamCounts[Int(sentence) - 1]
            if count == 0 { continue }
            if let confirmed = confirmedSeams(value, seamCount: count), !confirmed.isEmpty { votes[Int(sentence)] = confirmed }
        }
        return votes
    }

    /// The seams as the chat model sees them: N.M: "left" | "right".
    public static func seamPrompt(_ sentences: [String], _ seamsBySentence: [[Seam]]) -> String {

        for (i, sentence) in sentences.enumerated() {
            for (j, seam) in (i < seamsBySentence.count ? seamsBySentence[i] : []).enumerated() {
                let (left, right) = sides(sentence, seam)
                lines.append("\(i + 1).\(j + 1): \"\(left)\" | \"\(right)\"")
            }
        }
        return lines.joined(separator: "\n")
    }

    /// seam, bare of trailing commas; none for one word or too many.
    public static func leadWords(_ sentence: String, _ seams: [Seam], rules: SorterRules) -> [String] {

        let words = matches(word, before)
        guard words.count >= 2, words.count <= rules.leadWordsMax else { return [] }
        return words.map { w in
            let bare = JS.replace(w, leadWordTail, with: "")
            return bare.isEmpty ? w : bare
        }
    }

    /// The lead words as the chat model sees them: N: 1 word | 2 word.
    public static func leadPrompt(_ sentences: [String], _ seamsBySentence: [[Seam]], rules: SorterRules) -> String {
        var lines: [String] = []
        for (i, sentence) in sentences.enumerated() {
            let words = leadWords(sentence, i < seamsBySentence.count ? seamsBySentence[i] : [], rules: rules)
            if !words.isEmpty {
                lines.append("\(i + 1): " + words.enumerated().map { "\($0.offset + 1) \($0.element)" }.joined(separator: " | "))
            }
        }
        return lines.joined(separator: "\n")

    /// A starts vote: per sentence, a word number from 2 to the count offered.
    static func readLeadStarts(_ raw: JSValue?, leadCounts: [Int]) -> [Int: Int] {
        var starts: [Int: Int] = [:]
        guard let object = raw?.object else { return starts }
        for (key, value) in object.entries {
            let sentence = JS.number(key)
            guard JS.isInteger(sentence), sentence >= 1, sentence <= Double(leadCounts.count) else { continue }
            var word: Double?
            if let text = value.string {
                word = test(digitsOnly, JS.trim(text)) ? JS.number(text) : nil
            } else {
                word = value.number
            }
            guard let word, JS.isInteger(word) else { continue }
            if word >= 2 && word <= Double(leadCounts[Int(sentence) - 1]) { starts[Int(sentence)] = Int(word) }
        }
        return starts
    }

    /// Split a run's first piece where its first item begins.
    public static func splitLeadIn(_ piece: String, start: Int) -> (lead: String, item: String)? {
        guard start > 1 else { return nil }
        let source = piece as NSString
        let words = word.matches(in: piece, range: NSRange(location: 0, length: source.length))
        guard start <= words.count else { return nil }
        let at = words[start - 1].range.location
        let lead = JS.replace(JS.trim(source.substring(to: at)), leadTail, with: "")
        let item = JS.trim(source.substring(from: at))
        guard !lead.isEmpty, !item.isEmpty else { return nil }
        return (lead, item)
    }

    /// Cut a sentence at its confirmed seams and, when that makes a run,
    /// take the lead-in the model pointed at and trim the run's final period.
    public static func cutRun(_ sentence: String, _ seams: [Seam], confirmed: [Int], start: Int?, rules: SorterRules) -> (lead: String?, pieces: [String]) {
        var pieces = cut(sentence, seams, confirmed: confirmed)
        if pieces.count < 2 { return (nil, pieces) }
        let last = pieces.count - 1
        if !test(abbreviation, pieces[last]) {
            let trimmed = JS.trim(JS.replace(pieces[last], finalPeriod, with: ""))
            if !trimmed.isEmpty { pieces[last] = trimmed }
        }
        let offered = leadWords(sentence, seams, rules: rules).count
        guard let start, start <= offered, let split = splitLeadIn(pieces[0], start: start) else { return (nil, pieces) }
        pieces[0] = split.item
        return (split.lead, pieces)
    }

    /// Task and idea sentences with a confirmed vote, expanded into their
    /// pieces with the label kept; everything else passes through.
    public static func applySplits(
        _ sentences: [String], _ labels: [SortLabel], _ seamsBySentence: [[Seam]], votes: [Int: [Int]], starts: [Int: Int] = [:], group: String = "run", rules: SorterRules
    ) -> (sentences: [String], labels: [SortLabel], leads: [LeadIn?]) {

            if label != .note, let confirmed = votes[i + 1], !confirmed.isEmpty {

                    out.labels.append(label)
                    out.leads.append(lead)

            out.sentences.append(sentence)
            out.labels.append(label)
            out.leads.append(nil)
        }
        return out

    /// A markdown link from the folder holding fromFile to toFile, every
    /// segment percent-encoded as encodeURIComponent does.
    public static func relativeLink(from fromFile: String, to toFile: String) -> String {
        // Split on code units, as JavaScript does: a slash before a
        // combining mark is one Character in Swift.
        let fromDir = Array((fromFile as NSString).components(separatedBy: "/").filter { !$0.isEmpty }.dropLast())
        let to = (toFile as NSString).components(separatedBy: "/").filter { !$0.isEmpty }
        var common = 0
        while common < fromDir.count, common < to.count - 1, JS.same(fromDir[common], to[common]) { common += 1 }
        let ups = Array(repeating: "..", count: fromDir.count - common)
        return (ups + to[common...].map(encodeURIComponent)).joined(separator: "/")
    }

    static func encodeURIComponent(_ text: String) -> String {
        var out = ""
        for byte in text.utf8 {
            let keep = (0x41...0x5A).contains(byte) || (0x61...0x7A).contains(byte) || (0x30...0x39).contains(byte) || "-_.!~*'()".utf8.contains(byte)
            out += keep ? String(UnicodeScalar(byte)) : String(format: "%%%02X", byte)
        }
        return out
    }

    public static func planFiling(_ sentences: [String], _ labels: [SortLabel], time: String, inbox: String, tasks: String, ideas: String, leads: [LeadIn?] = []) -> FiledLines {
        var plan = FiledLines()
        let taskLink = relativeLink(from: tasks, to: inbox)
        let ideaLink = relativeLink(from: ideas, to: inbox)
        var open: [SortLabel: String] = [:]
        for (i, sentence) in sentences.enumerated() {
            let label = labels[i]
            if label == .note {
                plan.notes.append(sentence)
                continue

            let line = label == .task ? taskLine(sentence, time: time, link: taskLink) : ideaLine(sentence, time: time, link: ideaLink)

                    open[label] = lead.group

    }

// IOS-023): desktop's src/shared/compare.ts and pieces.ts in Swift. The

// numbered, and the model votes per sentence: new, the same as item N, or

    public static func context(tasks: String, ideas: String, cap: Int) -> [ContextItem] {
        var out: [ContextItem] = []

    public static func contextPrompt(_ items: [ContextItem], rules: SorterRules) -> String {

        let lines = items.map { "\($0.number). [\($0.done ? "x" : " ")] \($0.text)" }
        return header + "\n" + lines.joined(separator: "\n")

    /// The relations key: anything that is not a clean pair naming a real
    /// sentence and a real item means new for that sentence only.
    static func readRelationVotes(_ raw: JSValue?, sentenceCount: Int, itemCount: Int) -> [Int: RelationVote] {
        var votes: [Int: RelationVote] = [:]
        guard let object = raw?.object, itemCount > 0 else { return votes }
        for (key, value) in object.entries {
            let sentence = JS.number(key)
            guard JS.isInteger(sentence), sentence >= 1, sentence <= Double(sentenceCount) else { continue }

            guard let relation, JS.isInteger(item), item >= 1, item <= Double(itemCount) else { continue }
            votes[Int(sentence)] = RelationVote(relation: relation, item: Int(item))
        }
        return votes

    /// A note is compared for completion only: it files nowhere, so it is

    public static func pickRelated(_ sure: [Int], labels: [SortLabel], relations: [Int: RelationVote], items: [ContextItem]) -> (related: [RelatedLine], kept: [Int]) {
        var related: [RelatedLine] = []
        var kept: [Int] = []
        for i in sure {
            let label = labels[i]
            let vote = relations[i + 1]
            let item = vote.flatMap { $0.item >= 1 && $0.item <= items.count ? items[$0.item - 1] : nil }

            if compared, let vote, let item {
                related.append(RelatedLine(local: i, vote: vote, item: item))
            } else {
                kept.append(i)
            }
        }
        return (related, kept)

        item.done ? .doneBefore : vote.relation == .same ? .same : .completes

        guard label != .note, let confirmed = votes[local + 1], !confirmed.isEmpty else { return nil }
        let run = Sorter.cutRun(sentences[local], local < seams.count ? seams[local] : [], confirmed: confirmed, start: starts[local + 1], rules: rules)

        return PieceSet(local: local, label: label, pieces: run.pieces, lead: run.lead.map { LeadIn(text: $0, group: "\(group)-\(local + 1)") })
    }

    /// The related lines to compare piece by piece, and the ones held whole.
    public static func piecesToCompare(_ related: [RelatedLine], sentences: [String], labels: [SortLabel], seams: [[Seam]], votes: [Int: [Int]], starts: [Int: Int], group: String, rules: SorterRules) -> (cut: [PieceSet], whole: [RelatedLine]) {
        var cut: [PieceSet] = []
        var whole: [RelatedLine] = []
        for line in related {
            if let set = cutSet(line.local, sentences: sentences, labels: labels, seams: seams, votes: votes, starts: starts, group: group, rules: rules) {
                cut.append(set)
            } else {
                whole.append(line)
            }
        }
        return (cut, whole)
    }

    /// The kept lines that are runs, cut for the same piece-by-piece look.
    public static func runsToCompare(_ kept: [Int], sentences: [String], labels: [SortLabel], seams: [[Seam]], votes: [Int: [Int]], starts: [Int: Int], group: String, rules: SorterRules) -> [PieceSet] {
        kept.compactMap { cutSet($0, sentences: sentences, labels: labels, seams: seams, votes: votes, starts: starts, group: group, rules: rules) }
    }

    /// The pieces as one numbered list, each with its sentence's label.
    public static func flatten(_ cut: [PieceSet]) -> (sentences: [String], labels: [SortLabel]) {
        (cut.flatMap(\.pieces), cut.flatMap { set in set.pieces.map { _ in set.label } })
    }

    /// The second request's votes read back onto the pieces.
    public static func assignPieceVotes(_ cut: [PieceSet], relations: [Int: RelationVote], items: [ContextItem]) -> (held: [HeldPiece], filed: [FiledPiece]) {
        let owners = cut.flatMap { set in set.pieces.map { FiledPiece(local: set.local, text: $0, label: set.label, lead: set.lead) } }
        let flat = flatten(cut)
        let picked = pickRelated(Array(flat.sentences.indices), labels: flat.labels, relations: relations, items: items)
        let held = picked.related.map { r in
            let owner = owners[r.local]
            return HeldPiece(local: owner.local, text: owner.text, label: owner.label, vote: r.vote, item: r.item, lead: owner.lead)
        }
        return (held, picked.kept.map { owners[$0] })
    }
}

    public let done: Bool

    public let group: String
    /// A checkbox line; a plain bullet (the ideas file) cannot be ticked.
    public let checkbox: Bool

public enum Todo {
    /// Every checkbox line, in file order, fences and empty boxes skipped.
    public static func items(_ text: String) -> [ListItem] {
        var items: [ListItem] = []
        var group = ""
        var fenced = false
        for line in lines(text) {
            if Sorter.test(fence, line) {
                fenced.toggle()
                continue
            }
            if fenced { continue }
            if let heading = capture(headingLine, line) {
                group = JS.trim(heading)
                continue
            }
            guard let box = checkboxParts(line) else { continue }
            let itemText = JS.trim(JS.replace(box.text, trailingLink, with: ""))
            if itemText.isEmpty { continue }
            items.append(ListItem(text: itemText, done: box.mark != " ", group: group, checkbox: true))
        }
        return items
    }

    /// Every bullet, checkbox or plain, lead lines left out.
    public static func listItems(_ text: String) -> [ListItem] {
        var items: [ListItem] = []
        var group = ""
        var fenced = false
        let all = lines(text)
        for (index, line) in all.enumerated() {
            if Sorter.test(fence, line) {
                fenced.toggle()
                continue
            }
            if fenced { continue }
            if let heading = capture(headingLine, line) {
                group = JS.trim(heading)
                continue
            }
            if let box = checkboxParts(line) {
                let itemText = JS.trim(JS.replace(box.text, trailingLink, with: ""))
                if !itemText.isEmpty { items.append(ListItem(text: itemText, done: box.mark != " ", group: group, checkbox: true)) }
                continue
            }
            if let bullet = capture(bulletLine, line) {
                if isLeadLine(line, all, index) { continue }
                let itemText = JS.trim(JS.replace(bullet, trailingLink, with: ""))
                if !itemText.isEmpty { items.append(ListItem(text: itemText, done: false, group: group, checkbox: false)) }
            }
        }
        return items
    }

    /// Tick the nth checkbox, counted as items(_:) counts: the box's space
    /// becomes an x and nothing else changes. nil when it is not there;
    /// the text unchanged with done true when it was already ticked.
    public static func tick(_ text: String, nth: Int) -> (text: String?, alreadyDone: Bool) {
        var parts = splitKeepingBreaks(text)
        var count = -1
        var fenced = false
        for i in stride(from: 0, to: parts.count, by: 2) {
            let line = parts[i]
            if Sorter.test(fence, line) {
                fenced.toggle()
                continue
            }
            if fenced { continue }
            guard let box = checkboxParts(line) else { continue }
            if JS.trim(JS.replace(box.text, trailingLink, with: "")).isEmpty { continue }
            count += 1
            if count != nth { continue }
            if box.mark != " " { return (nil, true) }
            let source = line as NSString
            let at = source.range(of: "[ ]", options: .literal).location
            parts[i] = source.replacingCharacters(in: NSRange(location: at, length: 3), with: "[x]")
            return (parts.joined(), false)
        }
        return (nil, false)
    }

    /// One line for a reminder: the count, then as much of the next items
    /// as fits. Empty when nothing is open.
    public static func reminderBody(_ items: [ListItem], maxChars: Int) -> String {
        let open = items.filter { !$0.done }
        if open.isEmpty { return "" }
        let count = "\(open.count) open \(open.count == 1 ? "task" : "tasks")"
        let countLength = count.utf16.count
        var parts: [String] = []
        var used = countLength + 2
        for item in open {
            let length = item.text.utf16.count
            let cost = parts.isEmpty ? length : length + 2
            if used + cost > maxChars { break }
            parts.append(item.text)
            used += cost

        if !parts.isEmpty { return "\(count): \(parts.joined(separator: "; "))" }

        guard room > 8 else { return count }
        let units = Array(open[0].text.utf16.prefix(room))
        return "\(count): \(trimEnd(String(decoding: units, as: UTF16.self)))…"
    }

    /// text.split(/\r?\n/)
    static func lines(_ text: String) -> [String] {
        let parts = splitKeepingBreaks(text)
        return stride(from: 0, to: parts.count, by: 2).map { parts[$0] }
    }

    /// text.split(/(\r?\n)/): lines and the breaks between them, alternating.
    static func splitKeepingBreaks(_ text: String) -> [String] {
        let units = Array(text.utf16)
        var out: [String] = []
        var start = 0
        var i = 0
        while i < units.count {
            if units[i] == 0x0A {
                let breakStart = i > start && units[i - 1] == 0x0D ? i - 1 : i
                out.append(String(decoding: units[start..<breakStart], as: UTF16.self))
                out.append(String(decoding: units[breakStart...i], as: UTF16.self))
                start = i + 1
            }
            i += 1
        }
        out.append(String(decoding: units[start..<units.count], as: UTF16.self))
        return out
    }

    /// String.prototype.trimEnd.
    static func trimEnd(_ text: String) -> String {
        var units = Array(text.utf16)
        while let last = units.last, JS.isSpace(last) { units.removeLast() }
        return String(decoding: units, as: UTF16.self)
    }

    static func checkboxParts(_ line: String) -> (mark: String, text: String)? {
        let source = line as NSString
        guard let match = checkbox.firstMatch(in: line, range: NSRange(location: 0, length: source.length)) else { return nil }
        let mark = source.substring(with: match.range(at: 1)).lowercased()

    }

    static func isLeadLine(_ line: String, _ lines: [String], _ index: Int) -> Bool {
        guard trimEnd(line).utf16.last == 0x3A else { return false }

        func indent(_ l: String) -> Int {
            l.utf16.count - trimStart(l).utf16.count
        }
        return Sorter.test(deeperBullet, next) && indent(next) > indent(line)
    }

    static func trimStart(_ text: String) -> String {
        let units = Array(text.utf16)
        var start = 0
        while start < units.count, JS.isSpace(units[start]) { start += 1 }
        return String(decoding: units[start...], as: UTF16.self)
    }

    static let anything = "[^\\n\\r\\u2028\\u2029]"
    static let checkbox = JS.regex("^\(JS.space)*[-*+]\(JS.space)+\\[( |x|X)\\]\(JS.space)+(\(anything)*)\(JS.end)")

    init(_ replies: [SortScenarios.Reply]) {

    }

    var asked: [(String, String)] {
        state.withLock { $0.asked }

        { system, user in

    }

public enum NoteSort {
    /// Sort one note, or, with only, the positions Sort again sends.
    /// Never throws: every outcome is a report.
    public static func sort(
        _ input: NoteSortInput, only: [Int]? = nil, memory: SortMemory, folder: some NoteFolder, rules: SorterRules, ask: SortAsk?
    ) async -> (report: SortReport, memory: SortMemory) {
        var memory = memory
        let units = Sorter.splitUnits(input.text)
        let all = units.sentences
        let positions = only ?? Array(all.indices)
        let sentences = positions.map { all[$0] }
        let unitLeads = positions.map { units.leads[$0] }
        if sentences.isEmpty { return (SortReport(outcome: .skipped, reason: "nothing to sort"), memory) }
        // Held and settled lines follow the note: a fresh note starts
        // empty, and a line sent again gets a fresh verdict.
        if memory.input?.id != input.id {
            let next = memory.nextHeldId
            memory = SortMemory()
            memory.nextHeldId = next
            memory.input = input
        } else {
            memory.held.removeAll { positions.contains($0.position) }
        }
        guard let ask else { return (SortReport(outcome: .failed, reason: "no connection"), memory) }

        let seams = sentences.map(Sorter.findSeams)
        let seamCounts = seams.map(\.count)
        let offerSeams = seamCounts.contains { $0 > 0 }
        let leadCounts = sentences.indices.map { Sorter.leadWords(sentences[$0], seams[$0], rules: rules).count }
        let offerLeads = leadCounts.contains { $0 > 0 }
        let tasksText = folder.read(rules.layout.tasks) ?? ""
        let items = Compare.context(tasks: tasksText, ideas: folder.read(rules.layout.ideas) ?? "", cap: rules.compareCap)

        var system = [rules.systemPrompt.joined(separator: " ")]
        if offerSeams { system.append(rules.seamsPrompt.joined(separator: " ")) }
        if offerLeads { system.append(rules.leadsPrompt.joined(separator: " ")) }
        if !items.isEmpty { system.append(rules.relationsPrompt.joined(separator: " ")) }
        var user = [Sorter.numberedList(sentences)]
        if offerSeams { user.append("Seams:\n" + Sorter.seamPrompt(sentences, seams)) }
        if offerLeads { user.append("Lead words:\n" + Sorter.leadPrompt(sentences, seams, rules: rules)) }
        if !items.isEmpty { user.append(Compare.contextPrompt(items, rules: rules)) }
        let reply = await ask(system.joined(separator: " "), user.joined(separator: "\n\n"))
        guard case let .content(content) = reply else {
            if case let .failed(reason) = reply { return (SortReport(outcome: .failed, reason: reason), memory) }
            return (SortReport(outcome: .failed), memory)
        }
        let reading = Sorter.validate(sentences.count, content, seamCounts: seamCounts, items: items.count, leadCounts: leadCounts)
        guard case let .labeled(verdict) = reading else {
            if case let .rejected(reason) = reading { return (SortReport(outcome: .rejected, reason: reason), memory) }
            return (SortReport(outcome: .rejected), memory)
        }

        // A chat model reports no confidence, so every line is sure.
        let sure = Array(sentences.indices)
        let picked = Compare.pickRelated(sure, labels: verdict.labels, relations: verdict.relations, items: items)
        let starts = verdict.starts ?? [:]
        let split = Compare.piecesToCompare(picked.related, sentences: sentences, labels: verdict.labels, seams: seams, votes: verdict.seams, starts: starts, group: "piece", rules: rules)
        var whole = split.whole
        var heldPieces: [HeldPiece] = []
        var filedPieces: [FiledPiece] = []
        var comparedKept: Set<Int> = []
        let keptRuns = items.isEmpty ? [] : Compare.runsToCompare(picked.kept, sentences: sentences, labels: verdict.labels, seams: seams, votes: verdict.seams, starts: starts, group: "piece", rules: rules)
        let compared = (split.cut + keptRuns).sorted { $0.local < $1.local }
        if !compared.isEmpty {
            let flat = Compare.flatten(compared)
            if let relations = await askRelations(flat.sentences, items: items, rules: rules, ask: ask) {
                (heldPieces, filedPieces) = Compare.assignPieceVotes(compared, relations: relations, items: items)
                comparedKept = Set(keptRuns.map(\.local))
            } else {
                for set in split.cut {
                    if let line = picked.related.first(where: { $0.local == set.local }) { whole.append(line) }
                }
            }
        }
        let wholePieces = Dictionary(split.cut.map { ($0.local, $0.pieces) }, uniquingKeysWith: { first, _ in first })
        let wholeLeads = Dictionary(split.cut.map { ($0.local, $0.lead) }, uniquingKeysWith: { first, _ in first })
        for line in whole {
            let lead = (wholeLeads[line.local] ?? nil) ?? unitLeads[line.local]
            memory.held.append(HeldLine(
                id: memory.nextHeldId, position: positions[line.local], text: sentences[line.local], label: verdict.labels[line.local],
                reason: Compare.heldReason(line.vote, item: line.item), pieces: wholePieces[line.local], lead: lead, match: line.item, snapshot: tasksText
            ))
            memory.nextHeldId += 1
        }
        for piece in heldPieces {
            memory.held.append(HeldLine(
                id: memory.nextHeldId, position: positions[piece.local], text: piece.text, label: piece.label,
                reason: Compare.heldReason(piece.vote, item: piece.item), pieces: nil, lead: piece.lead, match: piece.item, snapshot: tasksText
            ))
            memory.nextHeldId += 1
        }
        memory.held.sort { ($0.position, $0.id) < ($1.position, $1.id) }
        memory.settled = Sorter.settle(memory.settled, positions: positions, keptLocal: picked.related.map(\.local))
        let heldCount = whole.count + heldPieces.count

        // Filed in spoken order: a kept sentence whole or cut at its
        // confirmed seams, a compared sentence the pieces no vote tied.
        var expanded: (sentences: [String], labels: [SortLabel], leads: [LeadIn?]) = ([], [], [])
        for i in sure {
            if picked.kept.contains(i) && !comparedKept.contains(i) {
                let one = Sorter.applySplits(
                    [sentences[i]], [verdict.labels[i]], [seams[i]],
                    votes: verdict.seams[i + 1].map { [1: $0] } ?? [:], starts: starts[i + 1].map { [1: $0] } ?? [:],
                    group: "run-\(positions[i])", rules: rules
                )
                expanded.sentences += one.sentences
                expanded.labels += one.labels
                expanded.leads += one.leads.map { $0 ?? unitLeads[i] }
                continue
            }
            for piece in filedPieces where piece.local == i {
                expanded.sentences.append(piece.text)
                expanded.labels.append(piece.label)
                expanded.leads.append(piece.lead)
            }
        }
        memory.topic = ""
        let written = write(input, expanded.sentences, expanded.labels, leads: expanded.leads, topic: "", folder: folder, rules: rules)
        let keptLabels = picked.kept.map { verdict.labels[$0] }
        switch written {
        case let .failure(failure):
            let landed = failure.landed
            let keptLocal = picked.kept.enumerated().filter { k, _ in
                let label = keptLabels[k]
                return label == .note || (label == .task && landed.tasks > 0) || (label == .idea && landed.ideas > 0)
            }.map(\.element)
            memory.settled = Sorter.settle(memory.settled, positions: positions, keptLocal: keptLocal)
            return (SortReport(outcome: .failed, tasks: landed.tasks, ideas: landed.ideas, notes: landed.notes, held: heldCount, reason: failure.reason), memory)
        case let .success(done):
            memory.settled = Sorter.settle(memory.settled, positions: positions, keptLocal: picked.kept)
            return (SortReport(outcome: .filed, tasks: done.tasks, ideas: done.ideas, notes: done.notes, held: heldCount, places: done.places), memory)
        }
    }

    /// Sort again: the newest note's unsettled lines only, or nil when none are left.
    public static func sortAgain(memory: SortMemory, folder: some NoteFolder, rules: SorterRules, ask: SortAsk?) async -> (report: SortReport, memory: SortMemory)? {
        guard let input = memory.input else { return nil }
        let pending = memory.pending
        guard !pending.isEmpty else { return nil }
        return await sort(input, only: pending, memory: memory, folder: folder, rules: rules, ask: ask)
    }

    /// The second, smaller request: the pieces against the same items. nil when it cannot be answered.
    static func askRelations(_ pieces: [String], items: [ContextItem], rules: SorterRules, ask: SortAsk) async -> [Int: RelationVote]? {
        let system = [rules.systemPrompt.joined(separator: " "), rules.relationsPrompt.joined(separator: " ")].joined(separator: " ")
        let user = [Sorter.numberedList(pieces), Compare.contextPrompt(items, rules: rules)].joined(separator: "\n\n")
        guard case let .content(content) = await ask(system, user) else { return nil }
        guard case let .labeled(verdict) = Sorter.validate(pieces.count, content, seamCounts: pieces.map { _ in 0 }, items: items.count) else { return nil }
        return verdict.relations
    }

    struct Landed {
        var tasks = 0
        var ideas = 0
        var notes = 0
    }

    struct WriteFailure: Error {
        let reason: String
        let landed: Landed
    }

    struct Written {
        let tasks: Int
        let ideas: Int
        let notes: Int
        let places: [String]
    }

    /// Tasks and ideas as lines with a link back, under the day's heading,
    /// tasks first; notes nowhere, the inbox already holds them.
    static func write(_ input: NoteSortInput, _ sentences: [String], _ labels: [SortLabel], leads: [LeadIn?], topic: String, folder: some NoteFolder, rules: SorterRules) -> Result<Written, WriteFailure> {
        let plan = Sorter.planFiling(sentences, labels, time: input.moment.time, inbox: input.inbox, tasks: rules.layout.tasks, ideas: rules.layout.ideas, leads: leads)
        var landed = Landed(notes: plan.notes.count)
        let heading = Sorter.filedHeading(rules.layout.heading, at: input.moment, topic: topic)
        var places: [String] = []
        let writes = [(rules.layout.tasks, plan.tasks, SortLabel.task), (rules.layout.ideas, plan.ideas, SortLabel.idea)].filter { !$0.1.isEmpty }
        for (file, lines, kind) in writes {
            let existing = folder.read(file) ?? ""
            let prefix = Sorter.headingPrefix(heading, existing: existing)
            let gap = !prefix.isEmpty && !JS.trim(existing).isEmpty ? "\n" : ""
            let tail = existing.isEmpty ? nil : String(decoding: [existing.utf16.last ?? 0], as: UTF16.self)
            do {
                try folder.append(file, NoteFiles.separator(tail) + gap + prefix + lines.joined(separator: "\n") + "\n")
            } catch {
                return .failure(WriteFailure(reason: "write \((error as NSError).code)", landed: landed))
            }
            if kind == .task { landed.tasks = lines.count } else { landed.ideas = lines.count }
            places.append(file)
        }
        return .success(Written(tasks: plan.tasks.count, ideas: plan.ideas.count, notes: plan.notes.count, places: places))
    }

    // MARK: Held lines

    /// File it anyway: land one held line, its pieces when it was cut.
    public static func file(_ id: Int, memory: SortMemory, folder: some NoteFolder, rules: SorterRules) -> Result<SortMemory, HeldRefusal> {
        guard let input = memory.input, let entry = memory.held.first(where: { $0.id == id }) else { return .failure(.notHeld) }
        if entry.label == .note { return .failure(.noteStays) }
        let lines = (entry.pieces?.count ?? 0) > 1 ? entry.pieces ?? [] : [entry.text]
        let written = write(input, lines, lines.map { _ in entry.label }, leads: lines.map { _ in entry.lead }, topic: memory.topic, folder: folder, rules: rules)
        if case .failure = written { return .failure(.writeFailed) }
        return .success(dropped(entry, from: memory))
    }

    /// Skip: the line stays in the inbox and is not sent again.
    public static func skip(_ id: Int, memory: SortMemory) -> Result<SortMemory, HeldRefusal> {
        guard memory.input != nil, let entry = memory.held.first(where: { $0.id == id }) else { return .failure(.notHeld) }
        return .success(dropped(entry, from: memory))
    }

    /// Tick it: the matched box in the tasks file becomes [x], every other
    /// byte kept, refused when the file changed in place since the match
    /// was read.
    public static func tick(_ id: Int, memory: SortMemory, folder: some NoteFolder, rules: SorterRules) -> Result<SortMemory, HeldRefusal> {
        guard memory.input != nil, let entry = memory.held.first(where: { $0.id == id }) else { return .failure(.notHeld) }
        guard let match = entry.match, match.file == .tasks, match.checkbox else { return .failure(.noBox) }
        if match.done { return .failure(.alreadyTicked) }
        guard let snapshot = entry.snapshot else { return .failure(.notRead) }
        let current = folder.read(rules.layout.tasks) ?? ""
        guard current.utf16.starts(with: snapshot.utf16) else { return .failure(.fileChanged) }
        let items = Todo.items(current)
        guard match.nth < items.count, JS.same(items[match.nth].text, match.text) else { return .failure(.moved) }
        let ticked = Todo.tick(current, nth: match.nth)
        guard let text = ticked.text else { return .failure(ticked.alreadyDone ? .alreadyTicked : .gone) }
        do {
            try folder.replace(rules.layout.tasks, text)
        } catch {
            return .failure(.writeFailed)
        }
        return .success(dropped(entry, from: memory))
    }

    static func dropped(_ entry: HeldLine, from memory: SortMemory) -> SortMemory {
        var next = memory
        next.settled = Sorter.settle(next.settled, positions: [entry.position], keptLocal: [0])
        next.held.removeAll { $0.id == entry.id }
        return next
    }
}

// the sorter's system and user prompts at shared/sorter.json's temperature

import Foundation

    /// The request, or nil when the address is not one the iPhone can call.
    public static func chatRequest(system: String, user: String, connection: PolishConnection, rules: SorterRules) -> URLRequest? {
        guard BaseURL.problem(connection.baseUrl) == nil,
              let url = URL(string: BaseURL.normalized(connection.baseUrl.trimmingCharacters(in: .whitespaces)) + "/chat/completions")

            Chat.Message(role: "user", content: user)

        request.httpMethod = "POST"
        request.setValue("Bearer \(connection.key.secret)", forHTTPHeaderField: "Authorization")
        request.setValue("application/json", forHTTPHeaderField: "Content-Type")
        let encoder = JSONEncoder()
        encoder.outputFormatting = [.withoutEscapingSlashes]
        request.httpBody = try? encoder.encode(body)
        return request

        { system, user in
            guard let request = chatRequest(system: system, user: user, connection: connection, rules: rules) else { return .failed("network") }
            let body: Data
            let response: URLResponse
            do {
                (body, response) = try await send(request)
            } catch let error as URLError where error.code == .timedOut {
                return .failed("timeout")
            } catch {
                return .failed("network")

            guard let http = response as? HTTPURLResponse else { return .failed("network") }
            guard (200..<300).contains(http.statusCode) else { return .failed("http \(http.statusCode)") }

                .flatMap({ ($0["message"] as? [String: Any])?["content"] as? String })
            else { return .failed("response missing text") }
            return .content(content)
        }
    }

            let content: String

// SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-3.0-only
// The notes folder on iPhone (ios/prd.json IOS-023): desktop's markdown
// files, the same layout and the same words. They live in murmur's own
// container, out of backups as history is, until you pick a folder of
// your own, such as an Obsidian vault, as desktop lets you. A picked
// folder is reached through the access iOS gave when you picked it, and
// every read and write is coordinated with the app or iCloud that also
// keeps it. Writes are atomic, so a note is either all there or not at all.
import Foundation
import MurmurCore

struct NoteStore: NoteFolder {
    let root: URL
    /// A folder you picked, kept by another app or iCloud.
    var picked = false
    /// A picked folder murmur can no longer reach: reads find nothing and
    /// writes fail, so a note is typed or kept elsewhere instead of lost.
    var lost = false

    static let standard = NoteStore(root: URL.applicationSupportDirectory.appending(path: "notes", directoryHint: .isDirectory))

    func read(_ relative: String) -> String? {
        guard let url = url(relative) else { return nil }
        return reachable { coordinated(reading: url) }.flatMap { $0 }.map { String(decoding: $0, as: UTF8.self) }
    }

    func append(_ relative: String, _ text: String) throws {
        try write(relative, merging: true) { (($0.flatMap { String(decoding: $0, as: UTF8.self) }) ?? "") + text }
    }

    func replace(_ relative: String, _ text: String) throws {
        try write(relative, merging: false) { _ in text }
    }

    /// The inbox days there are, newest first, as paths inside the folder.
    func inboxDays(rules: SorterRules) -> [String] {
        let folder = (rules.layout.inbox as NSString).deletingLastPathComponent
        guard let dir = url(folder) else { return [] }
        let names = reachable { try? FileManager.default.contentsOfDirectory(atPath: dir.path(percentEncoded: false)) }.flatMap { $0 } ?? []
        return names.filter { $0.hasSuffix(".md") }.sorted(by: >).map { "\(folder)/\($0)" }
    }

    /// A path inside the folder, never outside it.
    func url(_ relative: String) -> URL? {
        let parts = relative.split(separator: "/", omittingEmptySubsequences: true)
        guard !parts.isEmpty, !parts.contains(where: { $0 == "." || $0 == ".." }) else { return nil }
        return parts.reduce(root) { $0.appending(path: String($1)) }
    }

    private func write(_ relative: String, merging: Bool, _ next: (Data?) -> String) throws {
        guard !lost, let url = url(relative) else { throw CocoaError(.fileWriteNoPermission) }
        let scoped = picked && root.startAccessingSecurityScopedResource()
        defer { if scoped { root.stopAccessingSecurityScopedResource() } }
        try FileManager.default.createDirectory(at: url.deletingLastPathComponent(), withIntermediateDirectories: true)
        var failure: Error?
        var coordination: NSError?
        NSFileCoordinator().coordinate(writingItemAt: url, options: merging ? .forMerging : .forReplacing, error: &coordination) { url in
            do {
                let current = merging ? (try? Data(contentsOf: url)) : nil
                let text = next(current)
                try Data(text.utf8).write(to: url, options: picked ? [.atomic] : [.atomic, .completeFileProtectionUntilFirstUserAuthentication])
            } catch {
                failure = error
            }
        }
        if let error = coordination ?? failure { throw error }
        if !picked { try excludeFromBackup() }
    }

    private func coordinated(reading url: URL) -> Data? {
        var data: Data?
        var coordination: NSError?
        NSFileCoordinator().coordinate(readingItemAt: url, options: [], error: &coordination) { data = try? Data(contentsOf: $0) }
        return data
    }

    /// Runs body inside the picked folder's access; nil when it is lost.
    private func reachable<T>(_ body: () -> T) -> T? {
        guard !lost else { return nil }
        let scoped = picked && root.startAccessingSecurityScopedResource()
        defer { if scoped { root.stopAccessingSecurityScopedResource() } }
        return body()
    }

    private func excludeFromBackup() throws {
        var values = URLResourceValues()
        values.isExcludedFromBackup = true
        var folder = root
        try folder.setResourceValues(values)
    }
}

/// Which folder the notes live in, kept on this iPhone: murmur's own, or
/// one you picked, remembered as a bookmark iOS can open again later.
struct NotesFolderChoice {
    static let bookmarkKey = "notes.folderBookmark"
    static let nameKey = "notes.folderName"

    let defaults: UserDefaults

    init(_ defaults: UserDefaults = .standard) {
        self.defaults = defaults
    }

    /// The picked folder's name, or nil for murmur's own.
    var name: String? { defaults.string(forKey: Self.nameKey) }

    /// Remembers a folder from the picker.
    func pick(_ url: URL) throws {
        let scoped = url.startAccessingSecurityScopedResource()
        defer { if scoped { url.stopAccessingSecurityScopedResource() } }
        let bookmark = try url.bookmarkData(options: .minimalBookmark, includingResourceValuesForKeys: nil, relativeTo: nil)
        defaults.set(bookmark, forKey: Self.bookmarkKey)
        defaults.set(url.lastPathComponent, forKey: Self.nameKey)
    }

    /// Back to murmur's own folder.
    func forget() {
        defaults.removeObject(forKey: Self.bookmarkKey)
        defaults.removeObject(forKey: Self.nameKey)
    }

    /// The folder the notes live in now.
    func store(own: NoteStore = .standard) -> NoteStore {
        guard let bookmark = defaults.data(forKey: Self.bookmarkKey) else { return own }
        var stale = false
        guard let url = try? URL(resolvingBookmarkData: bookmark, options: [], relativeTo: nil, bookmarkDataIsStale: &stale) else {
            return NoteStore(root: own.root, picked: true, lost: true)
        }
        if stale {
            let scoped = url.startAccessingSecurityScopedResource()
            defer { if scoped { url.stopAccessingSecurityScopedResource() } }
            if let fresh = try? url.bookmarkData(options: .minimalBookmark, includingResourceValuesForKeys: nil, relativeTo: nil) {
                defaults.set(fresh, forKey: Self.bookmarkKey)
            }
        }
        return NoteStore(root: url, picked: true)
    }
}

// Notes on iPhone (ios/prd.json IOS-023): a take kept as a note instead
// of typed. The words land in today's inbox file first, as desktop's do,
// before anything else happens; then, with sorting on, a chat through
// your cleanup connection files the tasks and ideas the way desktop's
// sorter does (MurmurCore's NoteSort, held to desktop by
// shared/sort-scenarios.json). Lines the sort was not sure of are held
// for you, and kept across launches. One sort runs at a time.
import Foundation
import MurmurCore
import Observation

/// The newest note, for Home's row.
struct NewestNote: Codable, Equatable, Sendable {
    let id: UUID
    /// Its first line, cut to fit.
    let line: String
    let inbox: String
    /// Where a sort filed its lines.
    var filed: [String]
    let at: Date

    var place: String { Receipt.whereText(inbox: inbox, sorted: filed) }
}

/// What the notes keep between launches.
struct NotesState: Codable, Equatable, Sendable {
    var newest: NewestNote?
    var memory = SortMemory()
    var report: SortReport?
}

/// Where the notes' state is kept: beside the notes, out of backups.
struct NotesStateFile: Sendable {
    let file: URL

    static let standard = NotesStateFile(file: URL.applicationSupportDirectory.appending(path: "notes-state", directoryHint: .isDirectory).appending(path: "state.json"))

    func load() -> NotesState {
        (try? Data(contentsOf: file)).flatMap { try? JSONDecoder().decode(NotesState.self, from: $0) } ?? NotesState()
    }

    func save(_ state: NotesState) throws {
        let folder = file.deletingLastPathComponent()
        try FileManager.default.createDirectory(at: folder, withIntermediateDirectories: true)
        try JSONEncoder().encode(state).write(to: file, options: [.atomic, .completeFileProtectionUntilFirstUserAuthentication])
        var values = URLResourceValues()
        values.isExcludedFromBackup = true
        var excluded = folder
        try excluded.setResourceValues(values)
    }
}

@Observable @MainActor final class Notes {
    private(set) var state: NotesState
    private(set) var sorting = false
    /// Why the last held-line button did nothing, until the next action.
    private(set) var refused: HeldRefusal?
    /// Counts every change to the files, so screens that read them redraw.
    private(set) var revision = 0

    /// The folder the notes live in: murmur's own, or one you picked.
    private(set) var folder: NoteStore
    @ObservationIgnored let rules: SorterRules
    @ObservationIgnored private let stateFile: NotesStateFile
    @ObservationIgnored private let sortOn: @MainActor () -> Bool
    /// The chat a sort asks, through your cleanup connection; nil without one.
    @ObservationIgnored private let ask: @MainActor () -> SortAsk?
    /// Lays the reminders down again after the tasks file may have changed.
    @ObservationIgnored private let remind: @MainActor () async -> Void
    @ObservationIgnored private let timeZone: @MainActor () -> TimeZone
    /// Holds background time from iOS while a sort runs, so a note sorted
    /// just after you leave murmur still lands in its files.
    @ObservationIgnored private let keepAlive: @MainActor () -> @MainActor () -> Void
    @ObservationIgnored private var last: Task<Void, Never>?

    init(
        folder: NoteStore, rules: SorterRules, stateFile: NotesStateFile, sortOn: @escaping @MainActor () -> Bool,
        ask: @escaping @MainActor () -> SortAsk?, remind: @escaping @MainActor () async -> Void = {},
        timeZone: @escaping @MainActor () -> TimeZone = { .current }, keepAlive: @escaping @MainActor () -> @MainActor () -> Void = { {} }
    ) {
        self.folder = folder
        self.rules = rules
        self.stateFile = stateFile
        self.sortOn = sortOn
        self.ask = ask
        self.remind = remind
        self.timeZone = timeZone
        self.keepAlive = keepAlive
        state = stateFile.load()
    }

    var held: [HeldLine] { state.memory.held }
    var canSortAgain: Bool { !sorting && !state.memory.pending.isEmpty }

    /// Saves a note to today's inbox. Returns the word for the keyboard's
    /// receipt (inbox), or nil when the note could not be written, so the
    /// caller keeps the words some other way.
    @discardableResult
    func save(_ text: String, at when: Date = .now) -> String? {
        let moment = NoteMoment(when, timeZone: timeZone())
        let inbox = NoteFiles.inbox(moment, rules: rules)
        let entry = NoteFiles.separator(folder.read(inbox)) + NoteFiles.entry(text, at: moment, template: rules.layout.entry)
        do {
            try folder.append(inbox, entry)
        } catch {
            return nil
        }
        let input = NoteSortInput(text: text, moment: moment, inbox: inbox)
        state.newest = NewestNote(id: input.id, line: Receipt.line(text, max: rules.receiptLineMax), inbox: inbox, filed: [], at: when)
        keep()
        if sortOn() { queue { notes in await notes.run { memory, ask in await NoteSort.sort(input, memory: memory, folder: notes.folder, rules: notes.rules, ask: ask) } } }
        return Receipt.pillPlace(inbox, max: rules.pillPlaceMax)
    }

    /// Sends the newest note's unsettled lines again.
    func sortAgain() async {
        queue { notes in await notes.run { memory, ask in await NoteSort.sortAgain(memory: memory, folder: notes.folder, rules: notes.rules, ask: ask) } }
        await last?.value
    }

    /// Files a held line as it was labeled.
    func file(_ id: Int) async { await act { NoteSort.file(id, memory: $0, folder: folder, rules: rules) } }
    /// Lets a held line go without filing it.
    func skip(_ id: Int) async { await act { NoteSort.skip(id, memory: $0) } }
    /// Ticks the task a held line said was done.
    func tick(_ id: Int) async { await act { NoteSort.tick(id, memory: $0, folder: folder, rules: rules) } }

    /// Moves to another folder once the sort running now is done. Notes
    /// already written stay where they are.
    func use(_ next: NoteStore) async {
        await last?.value
        folder = next
        revision += 1
        await remind()
    }

    /// Waits for the sort running now, if any.
    func settle() async { await last?.value }

    private func act(_ body: (SortMemory) -> Result<SortMemory, HeldRefusal>) async {
        await last?.value
        switch body(state.memory) {
        case let .success(memory):
            state.memory = memory
            refused = nil
            keep()
            await remind()
        case let .failure(refusal):
            refused = refusal
        }
    }

    private func queue(_ step: @escaping @MainActor (Notes) async -> Void) {
        let before = last
        last = Task { [weak self] in
            await before?.value
            guard let self else { return }
            await step(self)
        }
    }

    /// One sort: the report and the memory it leaves, and the newest note's
    /// places when the sort was the newest note's.
    private func run(_ sort: (SortMemory, SortAsk?) async -> (report: SortReport, memory: SortMemory)?) async {
        sorting = true
        let release = keepAlive()
        defer {
            sorting = false
            release()
        }
        refused = nil
        guard let result = await sort(state.memory, ask()) else { return }
        state.memory = result.memory
        state.report = result.report
        if let newest = state.newest, newest.id == result.memory.input?.id {
            // In the order they were filed, each once.
            state.newest?.filed = result.report.places.reduce(into: newest.filed) { filed, place in
                if !filed.contains(place) { filed.append(place) }
            }
        }
        keep()
        await remind()
    }

    private func keep() {
        revision += 1
        try? stateFile.save(state)
    }
}

// Task reminders on iPhone (ios/prd.json IOS-023): desktop's reminders
// (US-052) say how many tasks are still open at the times you pick,
// reading the tasks file when each one is due. An iPhone app cannot wake
// to read a file, so the reminders are laid down ahead with the file as it
// stands, and laid down again whenever murmur changes it: a note sorted, a
// box ticked, a setting changed, a return to murmur. Only murmur writes
// the file on this iPhone, so what a reminder says is what the file says.
import Foundation
import MurmurCore
import UserNotifications

/// The notes' choices, kept on this iPhone: sorting, and the reminders.
struct NotesSettings: Equatable {
    static let sortKey = "notes.sort"
    static let remindersKey = "notes.reminders"
    static let timesKey = "notes.reminderTimes"
    /// Desktop's defaults (src/shared/settings.ts): sorting off, and
    /// reminders, once on, at 8:30, 13:00, and 17:30.
    static let standardSort = false
    static let standardTimes = ["08:30", "13:00", "17:30"]

    var sort: Bool
    var reminders: Bool
    /// HH:mm, in the order you set them.
    var times: [String]

    static func load(_ defaults: UserDefaults = .standard) -> NotesSettings {
        let times = (defaults.stringArray(forKey: timesKey) ?? standardTimes).filter(isTime)
        return NotesSettings(
            sort: defaults.object(forKey: sortKey) as? Bool ?? standardSort,
            reminders: defaults.bool(forKey: remindersKey),
            times: times.isEmpty ? standardTimes : times
        )
    }

    func save(_ defaults: UserDefaults = .standard) {
        defaults.set(sort, forKey: Self.sortKey)
        defaults.set(reminders, forKey: Self.remindersKey)
        defaults.set(times.filter(Self.isTime), forKey: Self.timesKey)
    }

    static func isTime(_ text: String) -> Bool {
        let parts = text.split(separator: ":")
        guard parts.count == 2, parts.allSatisfy({ $0.count == 2 }), let hour = Int(parts[0]), let minute = Int(parts[1]) else { return false }
        return (0...23).contains(hour) && (0...59).contains(minute)
    }
}

/// The reminders' pending notifications: one at each time on each of the
/// next days, all saying the tasks file as it stands now.
struct ReminderPlan: Equatable {
    static let prefix = "murmur.tasks."
    static let title = "murmur tasks"
    /// Send one now's reminder, which laying the week down leaves to arrive.
    static let now = prefix + "now"

    let items: [RecapPlan.Item]

    static func plan(tasks: String?, rules: SorterRules, settings: NotesSettings, now: Date = .now, calendar: Calendar = .current) -> ReminderPlan {

@MainActor enum TaskReminders {
    /// The last laying-down asked for; each waits for the one before.
    private static var last: Task<Void, Never>?

    /// Lays the plan down: its reminders added first, then every other
    /// pending reminder removed. With reminders off, nothing open, or
    /// notifications refused, every pending reminder goes.
    static func apply(_ plan: ReminderPlan, center: any RecapCenter) async {
        var kept = Set<String>()
        if !plan.items.isEmpty, await center.isAllowed() {
            for item in plan.items {
                await center.add(item, title: ReminderPlan.title)
                kept.insert(item.id)
            }
        }
        let stale = await center.pendingIDs().filter { $0.hasPrefix(ReminderPlan.prefix) && $0 != ReminderPlan.now && !kept.contains($0) }
        center.remove(stale)
    }

    /// A reminder two seconds from now, as one would read now, to see how
    /// it reads; what it said, or why nothing was sent.
    static func sendNow(center: UNUserNotificationCenter = .current()) async -> String {
        guard let notes = Notes.live(), let tasks = notes.folder.read(notes.rules.layout.tasks) else { return NotesCopy.noTasksToRemind }
        let body = Todo.reminderBody(Todo.items(tasks), maxChars: notes.rules.reminderMaxChars)
        guard !body.isEmpty else { return NotesCopy.nothingOpen }
        let content = UNMutableNotificationContent()
        content.title = ReminderPlan.title
        content.body = body
        content.sound = .default
        let trigger = UNTimeIntervalNotificationTrigger(timeInterval: 2, repeats: false)
        try? await center.add(UNNotificationRequest(identifier: ReminderPlan.now, content: content, trigger: trigger))
        return body
    }

    /// Plans the reminders again from the tasks file as it is now. Each
    /// waits its turn and reads the file and the settings only then.
    static func refresh(folder: some NoteFolder, rules: SorterRules, defaults: UserDefaults = .standard, center: (any RecapCenter)? = nil) async {
        let before = last
        let step = Task {
            await before?.value
            let plan = ReminderPlan.plan(tasks: folder.read(rules.layout.tasks), rules: rules, settings: .load(defaults))
            await apply(plan, center: center ?? SystemRecapCenter.shared)
        }
        last = step
        await step.value
    }
}

// New note (ios/prd.json IOS-023): one button to talk, as on Dictate, and

import MurmurCore

enum NoteTake {
    struct Kept: Equatable {
        let words: String
        /// Where the note landed (inbox), or nil when it could not be saved.
        let place: String?
    }

    /// A take that just came back as words is saved as a note; any other
    /// change saves nothing, so words already on screen are never saved twice.
    @MainActor static func keep(from old: TakeModel.State, to new: TakeModel.State, notes: Notes, at when: Date = .now) -> Kept? {
        guard old == .transcribing, case .words(let words) = new else { return nil }
        return Kept(words: words, place: notes.save(words, at: when))
    }
}

    @State private var notes: Notes?

        Group {

Tap New note on murmur's Home screen and talk. When you stop, the words are kept as a note instead of offered to copy, and murmur says Noted to inbox. An iPhone has no hotkey, so murmur gives you three quicker ways in, each opening New note already listening:

## How does murmur sort my notes on iPhone?

The same way as the Mac, with the same rules. Sorting is off until you turn it on: tap the gear, then Notes, then Sort into tasks and ideas. On, after a note lands, murmur asks your cleanup connection (Settings, Provider and key) to label each sentence a task, an idea, or a note. Tasks are copied to murmur/todo.md as checkboxes and ideas to murmur/ideas.md, each with a link back to the note; notes stay in the inbox. Nothing is reworded. A run like "I need milk, eggs, and bread" files as one lead line with each item under it.

murmur also checks a new task or idea against what is already filed. A line that repeats something filed, sounds like you finished an open task, or repeats something you already finished is held in the inbox instead of filed twice. Notes, under Held for you, shows each held line and what it was tied to, with up to three choices: Tick it marks the finished task done in todo.md (one box, nothing else in the file changes), File it anyway files it as labeled, and Skip leaves it in the inbox.

With sorting off, notes stay in the inbox only, and the words of a note never leave your phone for sorting. With no cleanup connection, or when the request fails, the note stays in the inbox and Notes offers Sort again. Your iPhone's sorter is a chat; the Mac can also use a decision model, which the iPhone does not.

## Can murmur save my notes to Obsidian on iPhone?

Yes. Your notes are plain markdown files, so any folder works, an Obsidian vault included. Tap the gear, then Notes, then Choose folder, and pick your vault. On iPhone, Obsidian keeps a vault that syncs through iCloud in iCloud Drive, then Obsidian, then your vault's name, and any other vault (Obsidian Sync, for one) in On My iPhone, then Obsidian, then your vault's name (Obsidian's help, checked 2026-10-14). murmur writes there what it writes on a Mac with the same folder: murmur/inbox for each day's notes, murmur/todo.md, and murmur/ideas.md. Point the Mac at the same vault and both add to the same files.

If both devices add to the same day's file before they sync, a vault in iCloud Drive can keep only one of the two versions. Obsidian Sync merges the two, except when both devices start the same day's file within a couple of minutes of each other, when it keeps one (Obsidian's help, checked 2026-10-14). Either way the words are still in that device's takes. Notes already written stay where they were: switching folders moves nothing. To go back, tap Keep notes in murmur. If murmur can no longer reach the folder, say after the vault moved, Settings, Notes says so, and a note from the keyboard is typed instead until you choose the folder again.

## Where are my notes kept on iPhone?

In murmur's own storage on your iPhone, out of iCloud backups like your takes, unless you choose a folder of your own (see Can murmur save my notes to Obsidian on iPhone?). The Notes screen lists your tasks, your ideas, and every inbox day; open any of them to read it as murmur wrote it, and tap Share to send it as text to Notes, Files, Mail, or anywhere else. The words of a note go to your cleanup connection only when sorting is on. Home's Notes row shows your newest note's first line and where it landed.

## How do task reminders work on iPhone?

Tap the gear, then Notes, and turn on Task reminders. murmur asks to send notifications only then. At each of three times you pick (8:30 AM, 1:00 PM, and 5:30 PM until you change them, as on the Mac), a "murmur tasks" notification says how many tasks are still open in todo.md and names the next few, the way the Mac's reminders do. With nothing open, nothing arrives. Send one now shows how one reads right away.

An iPhone app cannot wake up to read a file, so murmur sets the coming week's reminders ahead with your tasks as they stand, and sets them again whenever murmur changes your tasks (a note sorted, a box ticked, or a time changed) and each time you open murmur. A box you tick in Obsidian or on your Mac shows in the reminders after you next open murmur. After a week without opening murmur they pause until you open it again.
